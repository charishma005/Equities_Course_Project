# Results summary

Generated by `python -m src.report` from `results/tables/`. Mean-variance books, dollar neutral, 5% ex-ante active risk, covariance shrunk 50% toward its diagonal. Windows are by formation month: Common = Jan 1995–Dec 2025 (all four strategies trade), Post-2010 = Jan 2010 on, Recent 18m = Mar 2025–Aug 2026 (low power; REV has 10 evaluable months). Newey-West t-stats use 6 lags. Alphas are net of 20 bp one-way costs.

## Draft executive summary (for the team to edit)

We tested whether industry-level analyst EPS revisions (I/B/E/S) predict next-month returns of the 49 Fama-French industries beyond industry momentum, combining the two signals with IC-based weights in a dollar-neutral mean-variance portfolio targeted at 5% active risk. Over 1985–2025 the revision signal's mean IC was 0.010 (Newey-West t 1.06), and the part orthogonal to momentum had an IC of 0.000 (t -0.05). Revision portfolios turned over about 174% of capital a month, and net of 20 bp their FF5+UMD alpha was -4.5% per year (t -5.83) over 1995–2025. The blended strategy's alpha against FF5 alone, +2.4% (t 2.21), falls to +0.2% (t 0.36) once UMD is added, and to 0.0% (t -0.02) after 2010: its return is momentum exposure. No strategy has a positive FF5+UMD net alpha with t ≥ 2 in the full, common, or post-2010 windows (highest t 1.55), and neither does any robustness setting (highest t 1.40). The only t above 2 is in the recent 18-month window (Blend t 3.40 on 10 months), too few observations for the regression to be informative. Revisions do not add alpha beyond momentum, and by our pre-registered criterion we conclude: **do not implement.**
